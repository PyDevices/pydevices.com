01Put them on a board

The board needs OmniPython firmware with LVGL, its board config and Wi-Fi. At its REPL, the first line puts every app on it, each with what it needs, and the second starts Home when the board starts:

import omip
omip.install("github:PyDevices/apps/apps")
omip.install("github:PyDevices/apps/apps/home/main.py", target="/")

Reset the board, and Home comes up with a tile for each app. To put on one app at a time, name its folder instead:

omip.install("github:PyDevices/apps/apps/clock")

Install an app has the board config line, CircuitPython's extra step, updating, and how to start in one app with Home behind it.

02Home and Watch

Home is the home screen: a tile for each installed app, on a tab per category. On a round or small screen, Home's main.py starts Watch instead: a watch face with the date, the battery and today's steps, then a wheel of the same apps. Swipe sideways for another face, and tap for the wheel.

03What there is

  • Music Synth Station, Backline, midiEngine, MIDI Controller and Stompbox
  • Listening Spotify, Bluetooth Receiver, the MP3 player and the USB sound card
  • Cameras and calls a video camera, object detection, a doorbell, a videophone and a walkie-talkie
  • On a desk Clock, Weather and Steps
  • The board Info, Settings, the REPL, the Shell, Touch and Battery

Every app installs on any board, and one that can't run there says why when you start it.

04On a computer

pip installs every app as the package apps, with the libraries they share:

pip install "omnipython-apps @ git+https://github.com/PyDevices/apps#subdirectory=apps"

The libraries alone (appkit, controlkit, touchkit and the rest) are one package, omnipython-applibs from PyPI or applibs from lib.omnipython.org, for writing apps of your own.
